## Row-label reference — a closed vocabulary, not a fixed output shape

The table below is the closed row-label template: the exact set of
`Work type` strings (and units) you're allowed to use, rendered blank as
a reference/checklist while you work — it is NOT the literal shape of
your final answer. Do not print all ~200 rows in your response, blank or
not. Copy a row's `Work type` string into your final output **exactly as
written here** — including any depth-range, size, or SINGLE/DOUBLE split
suffix — only for rows where you found a real QTY. Do not merge split
rows into one, paraphrase a label, drop a qualifier, or invent a
row/label not on this list (that's Case A instead — see field rules).
Omit a row entirely from your final table, and record it in your Case B
list instead, when you found no evidence for it — do not leave it in the
table as a blank row.

This is a union across every job in the corpus, so near-duplicate
rows across different jobs are expected (different depth ranges,
sizes, or an occasional source-data typo carried over from a real
sheet) — use the census, profile data, and analog jobs
(`dwg_corpus_lookup`) to judge which specific variant this job's
evidence actually supports. Never guess a depth range or size
qualifier you have no evidence for; flag it (Case B) instead of
picking one arbitrarily.
